## Problem 5 assets added by the student

- `images/p5/video1.mp4` and the 48 frames extracted from it into `images/p5/frames/` —
  "Slap In The Face, Punch, Blow", by KlausHausmann,
  [source](https://pixabay.com/videos/slap-in-the-face-punch-blow-7375/),
  [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/). Published 16
  January 2017, not marked as AI-generated. 1920x1080 at 25 fps; frames were extracted at
  1280x720. This clip was chosen because it is raw, unkeyed footage: the backing has real
  lighting falloff (luma 110 to 150 across the frame) and sensor noise. Two earlier
  candidates were rejected because both had been pre-keyed onto a flat digital green —
  `vid.mp4` ("Man, Hair, Green Screen" by Kmeel_com,
  [source](https://pixabay.com/videos/man-hair-green-screen-chroma-key-190965/), Pixabay
  Content License) and `video.mp4`.
- `images/p5/background.png` — background plate, derived from
  `images/p4/local_regions.png` ("Toronto Skyline at night 2024-08-23", by
  TheWxResearcher, CC0 1.0, credited above), upscaled to 3400x1661 so that it is
  2.66x the 1280-pixel frame width and can pan across the 48 frames. Replace this with
  your own photograph if you would rather not reuse a supplied image.
